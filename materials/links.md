## MedASR (LinkedIn post shared alongside the course files)

- **Post:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/fereshtehmahvar_medasr-medgemma-share-7407876962800656384-5rKF
  - Announces Google's MedASR as a "free, open-weight and customizable medical speech-to-text model" that pairs with MedGemma.
- **Model card:** https://huggingface.co/google/medasr. Also available on Vertex AI Model Garden.
  - Conformer architecture, ~105M parameters, v1.0.0.
  - Gated behind the Health AI Developer Foundations terms.
- **Relevance to the project:** **English-only training data**, so it is **not eligible** under the no-English rule. It's only useful as background on domain-specific ASR.
