| 9 | Spectrograms and Formants | F1 and F2 are the main cues for vowel identity: [iy], [ae], [uw] with their tongue positions. |
| 10 | The Log Mel Spectrum | Mel filter bank (finer resolution at low frequencies) + log compression. Pipeline: waveform frame → DFT → mel → log. |
| 11 | Architectures of Understanding | The length mismatch: "dinner" is 6 letters vs ~30–40 frames. Two paradigms: encoder-decoder (Whisper), and self-supervised pretraining + CTC (HuBERT). |

| 13 | Self-Supervision (HuBERT) | Cluster MFCCs into pseudo-phones → masked prediction pretraining → linear head + CTC fine-tuning (CNN layers frozen). |
